About Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a free endless runner made for short, skill-based arcade sessions. Your runner moves automatically through a neon world. Jump over barriers, collect energy orbs, and try to beat your best score.

The game runs directly in your browser, needs no account, and stores progress locally on your device. We aim to keep the controls simple, the experience fast, and the game accessible on desktop and mobile screens.

How to play

  • Press Space, Arrow Up, W, or tap the game to jump.
  • Use the on-screen Jump button on mobile.
  • Avoid glowing barriers and collect energy orbs.
  • Try again to beat your personal best.
