About Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a free, browser-based endless runner built for quick arcade sessions. Your character runs automatically through a glowing digital world. Jump over barriers, collect energy orbs, and challenge yourself to beat your personal best. Your local progress is saved in this browser on this device.

Play fairly and take breaks between runs. This game does not require an account and does not send your score to a server.
